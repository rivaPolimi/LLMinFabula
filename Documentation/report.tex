\section*{Abstract}
\end{center}
\textit{LLM in Fabula} is a new version of the famous party game Lupus in Fabula in which players in the same place can use their phones to make the game easier. \\ In this game, the key element to enjoy a session is having a good narrator leading the story and the flow of the game. \textit{LLM in Fabula} provide a LLM narrator that helps the players fully enjoy the game while it leads the session in a more balanced and engaging way. Developed has a mobile application, it makes the game accessible and enjoyable for people of all ages, as children do not need an adult to help them understanding the rules or narrating the story, since the LLM does it instead. Each phase of the session is managed by the Artificial Intelligence, that speaks from the host phone, showing how the use of this new technology can actually help connecting people sometimes.

\section{Executive Summary}
Nowadays, digital tools are reshaping the way people interact and connect with one another. This concerns also how they play and have fun together. If few years ago was strictly necessary to meet in order to play a game together, today everyone can easily connect with friends to do that, using a computer or even just a smartphone. Indeed, several applications were released in these past years to replace classical board games and help people play  remotely. 

However, people are starting to rediscover the authenticity of sharing these experiences physically together and not only behind a screen, even if traditional social games often face practical limitations. \\Lupus in Fabula, a game built on narration, deception and group dynamics, requires a skilled narrator to manage rules, timing and atmosphere. It often becomes difficult to find someone among the players that can or want to do that. Without this role, the experience can quickly lose its balance and engagement, especially for new or young players. 

This project addresses this challenge by introducing an AI-powered narrator that transforms the game into an accessible, engaging and always available experience. \\The solution is provided as a mobile application, so that each player can use only his smartphone. At the core of the app there is an LLM narrator designed to guide players through the game. Acting as an impartial storyteller, the narrator assigns roles, manages the phases and help building a new story every time. The app allows each player to focus only on strategy, social interactions and immersion. Different settings are introduced, in order to provide even more possibilities to customize each game session, adding also an easier and shorter version of the game for children.

From a UX point of view, the interface is very simple and clear, in order to focus players' attention on the social interactions in the physical shared space, more than on the screen.

Technologically, ....

The final goal of this project is to make Lupus in Fabula more accessible, engaging and enjoyable. The app aims to preserve the social essence of the game, while enhancing is playability, providing a new version of the game, that relies on AI implementation, without giving up on social interactions. It offers a modern reinterpretation of the classical game that enables players discover the potential of technology as an ally more than a substitute.  

From this starting point, the project could be developed more in the future, adding new settings and options to customize the experience and narration.

\subsection{Stakeholders}
This project addresses teenagers and young adults (14-25 y.o.) as the main users, as they are usually more interested in playing this kind of games. \\However, it considers also kids (8-14 y.o.) and adults (25+ y.o., especially parents or educators) as secondary users, as the game could actually be played by almost everyone.\\These three categories together form the set of stakeholders.

Based on these categories, three users archetypes were defined using the \textit{persona} methodology. Personas are a qualitative design instrument for identifying fictional, yet realistic, characters representing distinct user groups. By consolidating patterns, expectations, and behaviors, personas facilitate the interpretation of complex sets of users and guide the design choices keeping in mind the users' needs as the main starting point.

\subsection{Needs of the Stakeholders}
The users' needs are related to all stakeholders, even if some of them are more specific for some categories. 
Primary needs are listed below with some sub-needs where needed to clarify and better explain the challenge.
\\
\\Users needs:
\begin{itemize}
    \item EMOTIONAL AND NARRATIVE IMMERSION: Users seek an engaging and evolving narrative experience capable of sustaining attention over time. Emotional involvement enhances participation and fosters a deeper connection with the game dynamics.
    \begin{itemize} 
    \item SUB-NEED: Be stimulated with ever-changing experiences
    \end{itemize}
    \item FUN: The experience is expected to be entertaining and engaging, encouraging active participation rather than passive listening. Enjoyment is a key factor in maintaining motivation and repeated use.
    \begin{itemize} 
    \item SUB-NEED: Be an active player and not a narrator: Sometimes being the narrator can be exciting, but when someone is not able or does not want to have this role, it can become something that slows down the game rhythm and discourage players to do multiple sessions.
    \end{itemize}
    \item POSSIBILITY TO PLAY THE GAME ONLY WITH THE SMARTPHONE: Users require the game to be fully playable using a single device, without the need for additional tools or materials. This ensures accessibility, portability, and ease of use in different contexts.
    \item CLARITY AND SIMPLICITY: Clear rules and intuitive interactions are essential to ensure immediate understanding of the gameplay. Simplicity reduces cognitive load and allows users to focus on the experience rather than on instructions. (Especially for young players)
    \item SAFETY AND APPROPRIATENESS: The content and interactions must be suitable for users of different ages and backgrounds. Ensuring safety and appropriateness contributes to a comfortable and inclusive gaming environment. (Especially for young players)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Context}
The project refers to the context of RPG (Role Play Games), as it is a revisitation of one of the most popular of those. It concerns people's free time as it is developed to be a way of entertainment, a fun activity. 

One of the core elements of this project is to maintain the social interactions that are characteristic of the original Lupus in Fabula game, so it is important that the app is used by people that are together in the same physical space while playing, but it can be used anywhere at anytime, since players only need their phones and no others supplies. 

The LLM narrator gives the possibility to each player to play, reducing the total minimun number of players compared to the original game, in which one of the players must take the role of the narrator (meaning that one more person is required to reach the minimun number). Anyway, to make the game session more engaging, this version is designed for at least 5 players, also to maintain the social nature of this game.

\subsection{Constraints}
Constraints that have to be taken into account for the development of the project are listed below divided into categories.
\\
\\Technological costraints:
\begin{itemize}
    \item SMARTPHONE: each player needs a phone with the app downloaded
    \item INTERNET CONNECTION: internet connection is necessary for each player to connect with others and join the lobby
\end{itemize}
\\
Social constraints:
\begin{itemize}
    \item GROUP SETTING: to play the game the expected and necessary minimum number of players is 5. The app is developed to host a maximum number of players in the lobby. 
\end{itemize}
\\
Resources constraints:
\begin{itemize}
    \item LIMITED RESOURCES: the project has to be developed in a limited time span, without a specific budget designated for that.
\end{itemize}
\\
Time constraints:
\begin{itemize}
    \item LIMITED TIME: each game session has to conclude within a limited time window in order to match limited attention span and engagment rate of players.
\end{itemize}

\section{Solution - UX Design}
This chapter describes the solution proposed and the design process for developing the User Experience and User Interface. 
\subsection{General approach}
The proposed solution is a mobile application that helps people play Lupus in Fabula in a more engaging way, providing a virtual narrator. 
\\In the next pages the design process and the motivations behind every choice will be addressed and explained in details. It is important to keep in mind that while working on the UX design, it was crucial to remember everything mentioned in Chapter 2: the requirements served as a guide during the entire process. 

CHOICE OF THE TECHNOLOGICAL SUPPORT \\In order to make the game more accessible for everyone to play it everywhere, the solution was developed as a mobile application for smartphones. In our society, the smartphone is the tool that everyone has with them everyday, representing an easy way to play the game without additional supplies. It is accessible also by children who can easily ask their parents' permission to use their phones.

CHOICE OF THE DESIGN STYLE \\One of the main goals of this project is to look at the technology as a support for already existing social dynamics. Starting from this assumption, the design of the user interface has been developed to be useful and intuitive, but not too rich. This was a choice made so that the screen will not be the main focus in the context of interaction with other players, that remains the core of playing together and having fun with others.

\subsubsection{Pre-Setting}
For what concerns the Pre-setting phase, the solution provide a faster and easier alternative to the classical game: the only task that the user has to complete is to gather with friends in the same place with their smartphones. The use of the app eliminates the struggle to convince some of the players to play as narrator and the difficulty to remember all the roles and rules (if the players do not have the board game cards with them at the moment, which is very commen due to the spontaneous nature of Lupus In Fabula game sessions).
\subsubsection{Game-Setting}
\begin{figure} [H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=1\linewidth]{Documentation/Images/GAME SETTING - TASK diagram.jpg}
    \caption{Game-Setting}
    \label{Game-Setting}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Opening the application, the user can simply tap on the screen to launch it and play with friends.
    \item The user can now choose to host the game, creating a new lobby, or join an existing one created by their friend. The choice is made by tapping on the corresponding button.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item HOST-CREATE LOBBY\\The host has to: insert the number of players, select the setting of the game among various versions available (Classical, Vampire, Zombie, Children), create and share a password for the lobby, choose his nickname for the game.
        It is also possible for the host to customize the settings of the game by choosing which roles to add. Each role is explained in an additional page that can be open when needed, pressing the button "roles". Secondary roles can be added only one per type.
        \footnote{In children version, in order to make the game easier and shorter, the host can not choose what roles to add and the children will play the game only with primary roles: wolves and villagers, automatically counted based on the number of players.}
        \begin{figure} [H]
            \centering
            \includegraphics[width=0.75\linewidth]{Documentation/Images/versions icon.pdf}
            \caption{Versions options (icons)}
            \label{Versions options (icons)r}
        \end{figure}
        \item GUEST-JOIN LOBBY\\The guests have to join the lobby created by their (host) friend by inserting the shared password and choosing their nicknames.
    \end{itemize}
    \item Only when the number of participants in the lobby reaches the number of players indicated previously by the host (meaning the everybody succeded in connecting to the lobby), it will be possible to select the button "start the game" and launch the session.
    \item Finally, the narrator presents himself, sets the mood to start the game and prepares players to recieve their role. When everyone is ready to play, they can tap on the screen and be able to discover their role in the game. 
    \item The players can visualize their roles on their screens. They will find also an explanation of their powers, their goal and what actions they have to do in order to win the game.\footnote{Roles are randomly assigned, so that everyone can have the chance to play different roles and interpret different characters in different game sessions. It is important that each player keeps their role secret in order to enjoy the mystery of the game, as in the original version of Lupus in Fabula} This phase is regulated by a timer, automatically controlled by the app, to give everyone time to read and understand their position but, at the same time, maintain a proper and engaging timing.
\end{itemize}
